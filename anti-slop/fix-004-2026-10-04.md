# Orders desktop/mobile refinement

Scope: local development UI, `src/app/orders/page.tsx`. No backend/schema changes, production deployment, push, database writes, payment submissions, or delivery mutations. Pre-existing unrelated changes preserved.

## Direction and decisions

- HATO emerald/slate operational interface; production screenshot guides grouping and information density, not unsupported edit/delete controls. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
- Bounded header groups selected week and create action. Existing Geist typography and 12px panels preserve identity; no decorative eyebrow, new assets, illustration, gradients, or motion.
- Four aggregates summarize the whole selected week, not filtered results. Active rows exclude cancelled orders; `sumMoney` sums exact decimal strings. Null payment balances are excluded from known totals and accompanied by an incomplete-data warning.
- Summary uses two columns on narrow screens and four when content fits. Payment colors communicate received/outstanding values; numbers remain tabular.
- Search and payment filters share one toolbar, with a visible result count and reset action for an empty search. Search covers name, group, phone, and historical product names.
- Desktop uses a seven-column semantic table; smaller containers use complete cards with products, quantities, snapshot unit prices, price type when supplied, totals, outstanding balance, payment status, and fulfillment state.
- Shared OrderItems/payment-badge components prevent card/table information drift. Native table scrolling contains any unusually wide content.
- Fulfillment labels come from real orderStatus values. Cancelled rows remain visibly labelled under Semua, and are excluded from payment filters; no fulfillment controls were added.
- Read-only create/submit guards remain. Cycle switches clear old rows and invalidate older requests so a stale response cannot replace the newly selected week's data.
