# Group targets, editable history and full payments

The manager confirmed two rules: one group target represents goods received, without a per-product stock split; correcting a fully paid order keeps it paid at the corrected bill total.

The web Barang page now compares group target/received quantity with recorded orders and shows the remaining amount or excess orders. There is no separate receipt-entry or supplier-allocation step in the web flow. Dashboard and cycle detail explain the same rule. Existing receipt and supplier records are retained; no schema migration or historical data conversion is performed.

Pesanan now exposes Edit, Hapus and Tandai lunas directly. Existing orders can be corrected or removed after the period ends or manual closure, including imported and delivered orders. Existing line prices are preserved, including historical products missing from weekly price rows. New lines require a valid configured price. Corrections preserve before/after quantities, values and payment evidence in audit/history. Deleted orders are omitted from active totals and piutang and can be restored through the Dihapus filter. Restoring a deleted order returns it as unpaid.

Full settlement locks the order, checks its version and computes the amount on the server. It is idempotent, rejects cancelled/unpriced orders and supports explicit historical full-payment confirmation without inventing a receipt date. A paid order whose bill changes has its old effective ledger entries reversed with a correction reason and a replacement full corrected amount recorded atomically. This implements the manager's requested bookkeeping rule; it does not perform a bank transfer or physical refund. Deletion reverses effective payment entries and keeps their history.

Piutang displays outstanding orders with one full-payment action. Public amount-entry/opening-balance endpoints reject new partial payments; existing partial history is preserved and can be settled in full. Unverified historical balances remain identified separately and can be confirmed from Pesanan. Pricing drafts still require price locking before payment. New order entry and fulfillment no longer depend on product receipt/allocation capacity.

Validation: 38 domain/contract/payment/order/reader checks plus 5 cycle/week-start cases passed on isolated loopback synthetic databases. Coverage includes concurrent settlement, replay, stale versions, historical prices, paid corrections up and down, deletion/restoration, demand and receivables updates, partial-payment rejection and distribution without receipts. A discovered raw PostgreSQL serialization error was added to the existing bounded transaction retries; concurrent distinct orders now both succeed. Changed-file lint and Next.js production build passed. Production records were not edited to test the change. Native screens were not redesigned or published in this web release.
